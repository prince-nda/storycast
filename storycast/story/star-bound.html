Star Bound

  • By StoryCorps
  • Animated short
  • About 3 min

An uncle and his nephew with stars in their eyes. This is a real conversation recorded by StoryCorps, the non-profit that preserves the stories of everyday people, and retold as a short animation.

Read the transcript

Joey Jefferson: Why do you like space so much?

Jerry Morrison: Uh, there’s so much sights to see: nebulas, hot jupiters and supernova remnants.

Joey Jefferson: Yeah.

Jerry Morrison: They look so beautiful.

Joey Jefferson: You know how I fell in love with space? My mom gave me a really cool space shuttle. You would wind it back, and then…

Jerry Morrison: Oh, I have that!

Joey Jefferson: You have that?! So, I remember playing with that all the time, and I wanted to become a pilot. I used to fly planes when I was 17 years old. And then after that, I started commanding spacecraft at NASA.

Jerry Morrison: Have you ever been to space?

Joey Jefferson: I have not been to space, but it’s a dream of mine.

Jerry Morrison: I want to live on another planet.

Joey Jefferson: Another planet?

Jerry Morrison: Like, what kind of planet would you live on?

Joey Jefferson: (Sighs) Of course everybody’s going to say Mars, right? Are you ’gonna say Mars?

Jerry Morrison: No. Kepler-452b.

Joey Jefferson: Oh, yeah. So Kepler-452b is your favorite planet. Do you know what we call those?

Jerry Morrison: Exoplanets…

Joey Jefferson: And there’s, actually, we estimate to be trillions of galaxies out there. So there’s a lot of stars and a lot of exoplanets that we got to find. And so we need people like you to keep doing what you’re doing. And it’s one thing to get to this place where you know all this knowledge, but it’s another thing to teach a knowledge.

Jerry Morrison: Yeah.

Joey Jefferson: So, you were in kindergarten and you taught the fifth graders, right?

Jerry Morrison: Yeah.

Joey Jefferson: How did you like that?

Jerry Morrison: It was a big opportunity for me. I, like, taught all the planets… It was awesome.

Joey Jefferson: (Laughs) How do you feel when we visit each other and we get to talk about space?

Jerry Morrison: It feels good. I learned from you a lot, like more than I could imagine.

Joey Jefferson: You’re my favorite person to talk about space to. You know that?

Jerry Morrison: Yeah.

Joey Jefferson: And you’re learning so much by yourself too, that you’re teaching me as well.

Jerry Morrison: Ha-ha…!

Joey Jefferson: And that’s really cool. The more you learn, the more we realize the little things in life we take for granted are the very things that make life possible. So when I look up in the stars, I think about that.

Jerry Morrison: That is pretty cool.

Joey Jefferson: My hope is that you are always going to be doing and learning about the things that you love the most. You can do whatever you want… but in the future, I think you’re going to go to Kepler 452b.
